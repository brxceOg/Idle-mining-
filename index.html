HOW TO PLAY
Start a run to send your workers underground. Each miner works its own column. Dirt earns $1, stone earns $2, and ore rewards increase with depth. Stop whenever you want: cash is kept and excavated terrain is restored.
MINER UPGRADES
Available cash: $0
Upgrades are permanent and remain after stopping a run. Resource Detection surveys the generated mine before the worker starts, then chooses a promising column while keeping the miner centered over each block.
Prototype note: run terrain is generated locally. Your cash and best depth are saved in this browser; saves do not sync between devices.
